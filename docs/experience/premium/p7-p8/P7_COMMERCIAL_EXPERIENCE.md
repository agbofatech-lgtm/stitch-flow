# P7 Commercial Experience

Shop invoice ≠ SaaS billing ≠ entitlement.

What exists:

- Settings `simulateTier` UX_ONLY, labelled Simulate
- FeatureGate UX presentation
- Control Center billing provider port

What does not exist and was not invented:

- Live card charge
- Production subscription activation UI
- Verified Postgres entitlement SoT in this UI
- Pricing amounts

Commercial workspace is therefore CONDITIONALLY the Settings simulation panel + Control Center billing plane, not a new commercial product area.
